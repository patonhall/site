Work benches and stools for hardware, prototyping and testing. Whiteboards and chalkboards along the opposite wall. A pool table at the back with a cap that drops to make it another work surface. Click any service on the left to read what it is and who can use it.

Pool Table

At the back of the room, open to anyone in the building during open hours. The cap drops over it to turn it into another large flat work surface, which is often what it is doing.

Space Rental

Book part of the floor for a stretch of work that needs more room than a bench, or that needs to stay set up. Requests go through the Calendar so bookings do not collide with events already scheduled.

Request a space

Event Rental

The whole room, for a talk, a launch, a demo or a class. Garage height and a bay door you can drive through, so what you are showing does not have to fit through a normal doorway.

Ask about an event

Blackboard & Whiteboard

A full wall of them, opposite the benches. Meant to be used and left up — working through something in front of other people is most of the point of the room.

Work Tables

Open tables for layout, assembly and anything that needs to be spread out. First come, cleared at the end of the session unless it is booked as an Assembly Zone.

Work Benches

Fixed benches with power and stools, set up for hardware, prototyping and testing. Shop members get priority on these.

Assembly Zones

A reserved area for a project that has to stay assembled between sessions, rather than being packed down every night. Priority goes to Shop and Keyholder members.

Tool Room

Shared hand and power tools kept in one place, signed out and put back. Some tools need a check-out from someone who has been shown how to run them.

Storage Bins

A labelled bin for parts and work in progress, so a project does not have to travel home with you. Keyholder members get extra storage.

Discord

Where the Hall talks between visits — asking for a hand, arranging build nights and posting what people are working on. Members get an invite.

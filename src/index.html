Hamilton has industrial heritage, technical culture, manufacturing capacity and a port. It's also the nexus for Toronto, Waterloo and Buffalo regions. Steeltown is the northern linchpin of the Great Lakes industrial corridor stretching from Ontario through the American Rust Belt.

This corridor and the wider Rust Belt are home to the reindustrialization movement. It aims to revive hard industries and bring advanced manufacturing to old towns. It's being driven by big policy and 1000s of small actors. The Hammer is the best positioned place in North America to accelerate experiments.

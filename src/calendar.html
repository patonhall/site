Wednesday October 21

EPTAC Course
IPC–A–610 Specialist

11:30am - 5pm
@ABCD

Description:
This 4-day lectured course is for anyone needing to obtain an operator-level, IPC-A-610 Specialist Certification. The lecture will review the content of the IPC-A-610 Standard and provide explanation of the accept/reject criteria, of all 3 product classes, to be applied to their visual inspection process.

Lectures will be covered over a 4-day period, with testing daily on the modules covered that day. Class length will vary daily based on the lectures covered and the testing time needed by each attendee.

Attendees must successfully complete open book exams in all 7 modules to obtain a full 610 Specialist certification.

All instruction and communication within the class will be conducted in English, and attendees need to have the ability to understand both spoken and written English to fully engage with the course material and complete the exams.

Contact: eptac@patonhall.ca

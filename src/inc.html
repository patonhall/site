Paton Hall Inc.

This is the formal sub-site for the Paton Hall Corporation, a Canadian company established to profitably operate the Paton Hall Hub, related business activities, and diverse local partnerships.

What

"Paton Hall" is a members-directed organization in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. It has been established to support individual development in the Industrial Arts that enable excellence in the professional areas of: electronics, machinery, robotics, collegiate education, product development, as well as the design, launching and operating of new enterprises for scale.

To these ends, Paton Hall operates industrial space(s) within Hamilton that allows members and the general public to work alone or in groups around the clock, undertake physical assembly projects, organize training, educational events, and social gatherings, as well as unwind and play games.

Where

4 Breadalbane St, Hamilton, Ontario

Why

The Paton Hall model to personal and economic development could be described, in simple terms, as a Hardware Hall or Hub. In the end, the organization is a shell and vessel for Members to direct themselves and their projects, acquire knowledge, build relationships and take action.

Although we do not claim any direct lineage to past organizations, we have been strongly inspired by the Mechanics' Institute model. Around 1800, George Birbeck in Glasgow began providing scientific instructions to people working in the mechanical trades. Birbeck's approach was notably preceded by the Spitalfields Mathematical Society and the Lunar Society of Birmingham. In 1821, Leonard Horner started the Edinburgh School of Arts for the Education of Mechanics. And by 1823, the Glasgow Mechanics' Institution was created. From there, the movement spread quickly around the world. The Hamilton & Gore Mechanics' Institute was founded in 1839 and also operated initially from a rented premise on King Street.

Paton Hall Inc.

This is the formal sub-site for the Paton Hall Corporation, a Canadian company established to profitably operate the Paton Hall Hub and related business activities, in Hamilton, Ontario.

How

The main jobs i.e. charges of the organization are to:

  • lease or own space suitable to the needs of a local Membership pursuing Industrial Arts
  • maintain all means by which a Membership can exist, fluctuate and self-organize
  • liaise with local industry and institutions to promote educational activities
  • promote rental of space to the public for special activities and events
  • broker access to electronics products and diverse types of hardware
  • actively maintain games tables and boards to encourage socializing
  • help bring attention to the works and inventions of Members

A rotating Board of Advisors works with the corporation's Executive and Board of Directors, to evolve and adapt operations to the needs and interests of the Membership.

This Advisory Board sits for 2 years, is composed of 6 people, with 3 of the 6 persons active for Year 1 while the other 3 persons shadow. In Year 2, the roles switch. If any Active Advisor can't fulfill their role for any reasons for a period of 3 months or more, the Reserve Advisors elect from among themselves a replacement. If a replacement can't be confirmed, the Executive will appoint a willing Member to become an Active or Reserve Advisor.*

* These formal processes have yet to be affirmed by Members and ratified by the Executive.
